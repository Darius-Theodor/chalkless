# Skill: ideen

Questions and long requests: `kern/regeln.md` §4.

## Steps
1. Identify area and topic; check STAND and the area's year/unit plan for where the topic sits and which curriculum part applies (cite it if `geprüft`).
2. Offer **three distinct approaches**, each: hook, core activity, how results are secured, material needed. Mark which fits their usual methods (`profil/stil.md`) and which is a stretch.
3. One differentiation idea and one picture or material idea per approach.
4. End with: "Welche soll ich ausarbeiten?"
5. Nothing is saved. Exception: they say "merk dir das" → add a row to `review/offen.md` as an idea note (no commit needed beyond that change).

## Tone
Short, concrete, seasonal, fitting the age group. No theory lectures.
